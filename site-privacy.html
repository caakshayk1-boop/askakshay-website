Privacy Policy

For askakshay.com — the website. Last updated 14 September 2026.

Short version: this site sets no cookies, runs no analytics and contains no advertising or tracking scripts. The only personal data it ever asks for is an email address, and only if you choose to subscribe to the newsletter.

1. Scope

This policy covers the website at askakshay.com only. The mobile applications published under Finance Suite Apps are covered by a separate policy at privacy.html; nothing described there applies to this website, and nothing here applies to those apps.

2. What is not collected

There is no analytics on this site — no Google Analytics, no Tag Manager, no Plausible, no Meta pixel, no session recording, no heatmaps. No cookies are set by this site, so there is no cookie banner to dismiss. No profile is built about you and nothing is sold or shared for advertising.

3. The newsletter

If you enter your email address in the subscribe box, it is sent to beehiiv, the newsletter platform that stores the list and delivers the emails on my behalf. beehiiv receives your email address and, as the sending platform, records ordinary delivery information such as whether a message was opened.

  • Your address is used to send the newsletter. Nothing else.
  • It is never sold, rented or passed to advertisers.
  • Every email carries an unsubscribe link, and unsubscribing removes you from the list.
  • You can ask me to delete your address directly — see Contact below.

4. The calculators

The break-even, runway, NPV, contribution-margin and viability calculators run entirely in your browser. The numbers you type are never transmitted anywhere, are not stored, and are gone when you close the tab. Nothing you enter reaches me or any third party.

5. Fonts and hosting

Typefaces are loaded from Google Fonts, so Google receives your IP address in order to serve the font files. This site is hosted on GitHub Pages and delivered through Cloudflare; both keep short-lived server logs containing IP addresses and request details, which they use to deliver the site and protect it from abuse. I do not have access to a per-visitor log and do not attempt to identify visitors.

6. Links out

Pages here link to other sites I run — among them signal.askakshay.com, gems.askakshay.com and terminal.askakshay.com — and to third-party sites. Once you follow a link, that site’s own policy governs what it collects.

7. Your rights

Because the only personal data held is a newsletter email address, requests are simple: you may ask what is held, ask for it to be corrected, or ask for it to be deleted. Write to the address below and I will action it.

8. Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top changes with it. Material changes will be noted in the newsletter.

9. Contact

Questions about this policy, or a deletion request: get in touch via the contact section.
